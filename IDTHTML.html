Carbon Footprint Calculator with Reduction Tips

Introduction

Carbon emissions are the release of CO₂ and other greenhouse gases caused by human activities such as electricity usage, transportation, and fuel consumption. These emissions contribute to global warming and climate change.

Problem Identification

  • Lack of awareness about daily carbon emissions
  • Complex and non-user-friendly calculators
  • No region-specific results
  • Limited guidance to reduce emissions

Methodology

Data is collected through surveys covering electricity use, travel, fuel, and waste generation. Emission factors are applied to calculate per-person carbon emissions.
