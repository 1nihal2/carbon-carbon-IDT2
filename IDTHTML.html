What is Carbon Footprint?

Your carbon footprint measures the total greenhouse gas emissions caused by your daily activities, including electricity usage, transportation, fuel consumption, and waste generation. Understanding your impact is the first step toward a sustainable future.

Simple Ways to Reduce Emissions

💡

Emission Prevention Tips

🔌 Switch to Renewable Energy

Use solar panels or switch to green energy providers to reduce electricity emissions by up to 80%.

🚌 Use Public Transportation

Taking public transport or carpooling can reduce your travel emissions by 45% compared to driving alone.

♻️ Recycle & Compost

Proper waste management reduces methane emissions and conserves resources for future generations.

💡 Energy Efficient Appliances

LED bulbs and Energy Star appliances can cut your electricity consumption by 25-50%.

🌳 Plant Trees

A single tree absorbs about 48 pounds of CO₂ per year. Plant trees to offset your carbon footprint.

🛒 Reduce Consumption

Buy local, reduce plastic use, and choose sustainable products to minimize your environmental impact.
